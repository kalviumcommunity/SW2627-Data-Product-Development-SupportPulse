# Business Visualization Documentation

## Project

SupportPulse – Customer Support Analytics Dashboard

---

# Chart 1: Revenue by Region

**Chart Type:** Horizontal Bar Chart

**Business Question**

Which regions generate the highest revenue?

**Why this chart?**

A horizontal bar chart makes it easy to compare revenue across regions.

**Key Insight**

North America generates the highest revenue, while Asia Pacific contributes the least.

**Annotation**

The region with the highest revenue is highlighted using an arrow.

---

# Chart 2: Monthly Revenue Trend

**Chart Type:** Line Chart

**Business Question**

How has revenue changed over time?

**Why this chart?**

A line chart is ideal for visualizing trends across months.

**Key Insight**

Revenue peaks during the highest-performing month and shows the overall business trend.

**Annotation**

The highest monthly revenue point is annotated.

---

# Chart 3: Transaction Amount Distribution

**Chart Type:** Histogram

**Business Question**

How are transaction amounts distributed?

**Why this chart?**

A histogram shows the frequency distribution of transaction values.

**Key Insight**

Most transactions fall within the middle range, while very high-value transactions are less common.

**Annotation**

A vertical dashed line marks the average transaction amount.

---

# Chart 4: Revenue by Month and Plan Type

**Chart Type:** Stacked Bar Chart

**Business Question**

How much revenue does each subscription plan contribute every month?

**Why this chart?**

A stacked bar chart compares monthly totals while showing the contribution of each plan type.

**Key Insight**

Premium plans contribute the largest share of revenue in most months.

---

# Chart 5: Monthly Spend vs Customer Revenue

**Chart Type:** Scatter Plot

**Business Question**

Is there a relationship between customer monthly spend and total revenue?

**Why this chart?**

A scatter plot helps identify correlations and outliers.

**Key Insight**

Customers with higher monthly spending generally generate higher total revenue.

**Annotation**

The customer with the highest revenue is highlighted.

---

# Design Principles Applied

## 1. Appropriate Chart Selection

Each visualization uses the most suitable chart type for its business question.

- Bar Chart → Category comparison
- Line Chart → Trend analysis
- Histogram → Distribution
- Stacked Bar Chart → Composition
- Scatter Plot → Correlation

---

## 2. Consistent Color Palette

Primary Blue (#1f77b4)

Secondary Orange (#ff7f0e)

Success Green (#2ca02c)

Danger Red (#d62728)

Purple (#9467bd)

The same palette is used across all charts to maintain visual consistency.

---

## 3. Labels

Every chart includes:

- Title
- X-axis label
- Y-axis label
- Legend (where applicable)

---

## 4. Annotations

Important insights are highlighted using annotations and reference markers.

Examples:

- Highest Revenue Region
- Peak Revenue Month
- Average Transaction Line
- Highest Revenue Customer
