# Business Language Translation

## Technical Version

We analyzed customer churn using support-related variables including resolution time, CSAT, and escalation frequency. Statistical modelling can be used to evaluate whether these variables predict churn.

## Business Version

We examined customer support experiences to identify signals that can help us recognize customers who may be at risk of leaving.

The strongest signals in our current data are customer satisfaction and repeated escalations.

Customers with low satisfaction have a 36.36% churn rate, compared with 21.05% among customers with excellent satisfaction.

Customers with two escalations have a 36.36% churn rate, while customers with three escalations have a 50.00% churn rate.

These patterns suggest that poor support experiences can be useful warning signals for the retention team.

## Communication Rule

The narrative focuses on:

- customer impact
- churn rates
- revenue impact
- operational actions
- measurable business outcomes

Technical statistical terminology is kept out of the main leadership narrative.
