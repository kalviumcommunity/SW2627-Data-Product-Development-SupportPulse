# Finding 1: Low Customer Satisfaction Is Associated With Higher Churn

## Finding

Customers with low customer satisfaction show a higher churn rate than customers with excellent satisfaction scores.

## Supporting Evidence

Source:

`supporting_evidence/csat_churn.csv`

The analysis produced the following results:

| CSAT Group | Customers | Churn Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Low CSAT | 11 | 36.36% |
| Below Average | 14 | 14.29% |
| Good | 57 | 22.81% |
| Excellent | 38 | 21.05% |

The Low CSAT group has a churn rate of **36.36%**, compared with **21.05%** for the Excellent CSAT group.

This represents a difference of approximately **15.31 percentage points**.

## Why This Evidence Matters

Customer satisfaction provides a measurable signal of customer experience. The higher churn rate among customers with low CSAT indicates that customers reporting poor experiences should receive additional retention attention.

This does not prove that low satisfaction directly causes churn, but it identifies a useful risk signal that can be monitored by the support and customer-success teams.

## Business Implication

Customers with low CSAT should be prioritized for proactive follow-up before they become lost customers.
