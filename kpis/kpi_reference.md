# KPI Reference Document

---

## KPI 1: Monthly Active Users (MAU)

**Definition**
Distinct customers who made at least one transaction in the last 30 days.

**Formula**
COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id) WHERE transaction_date >= TODAY() - 30 days

**Data Source**
transactions table

Columns:
- customer_id
- transaction_date

**Target Range**
5,000 – 6,000

**Owner**
Product Manager

**Update Frequency**
Daily

**Notes**
Measures product engagement and active customer usage.

---

## KPI 2: Revenue Per Customer

**Definition**
Average revenue generated by each unique customer.

**Formula**
Total Revenue / Unique Customers

**Data Source**
transactions table

Columns:
- customer_id
- amount

**Target Range**
$90 – $110

**Owner**
Finance Team

**Update Frequency**
Daily

**Notes**
Measures customer value and monetization.

---

## KPI 3: Churn Rate

**Definition**
Percentage of customers who became inactive during the evaluation period.

**Formula**
Churned Customers / Previously Active Customers

**Data Source**
transactions table

Columns:
- customer_id
- transaction_date

**Target Range**
0% – 5%

**Owner**
Customer Success

**Update Frequency**
Weekly

**Notes**
Lower churn indicates stronger customer retention.

---

## KPI 4: Payment Success Rate

**Definition**
Percentage of successful payments.

**Formula**
Successful Payments / Total Payment Attempts

**Data Source**
payments table

Columns:
- payment_status

**Target Range**
95% – 100%

**Owner**
Finance Team

**Update Frequency**
Daily

**Notes**
Measures payment gateway reliability.

---

## KPI 5: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Definition**
Average marketing cost required to acquire one customer.

**Formula**
Marketing Cost / New Customers

**Data Source**
marketing table

Columns:
- marketing_cost
- new_customers

**Target Range**
$0 – $50

**Owner**
Marketing Team

**Update Frequency**
Monthly

**Notes**
Lower acquisition cost indicates better marketing efficiency.
