# Feedback and Edits

## Reviewer

Reviewer: Peer / Mentor / Team Member

## Feedback Received

The analysis narrative was reviewed for clarity, business relevance, evidence, and actionability.

The main feedback was to:

1. Keep the narrative focused on business impact.
2. Support important findings with specific numbers.
3. Avoid technical statistical terminology in the executive narrative.
4. Avoid claiming that correlation proves causation.
5. Make recommendations specific by including an owner and timeline.
6. Clearly mention limitations caused by small sample sizes.

## Edits Made

### Added Specific Evidence

The narrative includes concrete figures such as:

- 120 total customers
- 27 churned customers
- 22.50% overall churn rate
- 36.36% churn among Low-CSAT customers
- 21.05% churn among Excellent-CSAT customers
- 36.36% churn among customers with two escalations
- 50.00% churn among customers with three escalations
- $1,750 revenue associated with churned customers

### Reduced Technical Jargon

Technical statistical terminology was removed from the main business narrative.

The story focuses on:

- customer experience
- churn risk
- support performance
- revenue impact
- operational actions

### Added Data Limitations

The narrative identifies that some resolution-time and escalation groups contain very few customers.

For example, the 2–4 hour resolution group contains only two customers, while the three-escalation group contains four customers.

These results are therefore presented as signals rather than definitive causal relationships.

### Improved Recommendations

The recommendations include:

- What should be done
- Why it should be done
- Expected impact
- Responsible team
- Implementation timeline
- Success measurement

## Final Assessment

The final narrative connects the analysis findings to practical business actions while keeping the explanation understandable for a non-technical audience.
